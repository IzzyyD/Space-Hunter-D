# Space Hunter D
Space Hunter D is a 2D action platformer inspired by games like Mega Man and Ratchet & Clank.

You play as D, a bounty hunter tasked with tracking down and eliminating Blackbeard and his 7 Pirate Lords who have taken over various planets in the Solaris System, plundering and killing all those who stand in their way.

Defeat them and use their insanely powerful and unique weapons to save the galaxy…and collect a lot of money!
